# AI Autonomy Zones

Status: CONTROLLED

## GREEN — autonomous
AI may implement tests, documentation, refactoring, safe bug fixes and non-breaking improvements inside approved contracts.

## YELLOW — human approval
AI may analyse and prepare API, protocol, data-model, dependency or cross-layer changes. Human approval is required before merge.

## RED — human decision
AI may analyse and propose, but cannot independently approve changes to the Project Constitution, foundational ontology, Trust model, fundamental security or governance rules.

## BLACK — STOP
AI must stop when it detects contradiction, invariant violation, unsafe or unknown state, or insufficient evidence. It must report the conflict rather than guess.

## Principle
RED means no autonomous decision, not no AI analysis. BLACK means no continued execution until the conflict is resolved.
